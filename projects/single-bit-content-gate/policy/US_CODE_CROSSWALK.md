## Logic and legal duties are different domains

The deployed pilot decision remains only `HOLD = A OR B OR C OR D OR E`. It is a *public-output decision*, **not** a substitute for statutory compliance, reports, appeals, reviewer safety, privacy, or a legal determination. XOR is used only to test the OR identity over 32 possible five-input states.
