# Proposed single-Boolean House/Senate bills: U.S. Code crosswalk

**As reviewed 2026-11-12.** This is an issue-spotting comparison, not a counsel opinion or certification of legal compliance. The House and Senate drafts have identical operative text, different chamber headers. The proposals are **not introduced or enacted**. The U.S. Code does not presently impose the proposed `HOLD` expression or this project's public-chart attestation.

| Proposed section | U.S. Code / source | Compatibility question and drafting response |
|---|---|---|
| § 2, non-coercion | 47 U.S.C. § 230; U.S. Const. amend. I | Platforms retain lawful moderation discretion. No federal universal Internet driver's license. This narrower approach reduces but does not eliminate speech concerns. |
| § 3–4, voluntary pilot | 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506 (COPPA) | COPPA's statutory child definition is under 13 and regulates data use, not the sample's broader colloquial terms. Do not confuse keyword age references with COPPA applicability. |
| § 4, Boolean gate | 18 U.S.C. § 2258A(f) | Current law expressly does not impose a duty to monitor or affirmatively search/screen by virtue of § 2258A. The proposed pilot is voluntary, and the gate is not a replacement for actual-knowledge reporting. |
| § 5, public chart and archives | 5 U.S.C. § 552; 44 U.S.C. § 3506 | These provisions concern agency public information and information-resource governance. Neither currently commands disclosure of this private project's PLC flowchart; proposed § 5 would establish a new specific pilot requirement. Protect private user data and exploit-sensitive incident details. |
| § 5(c), re-attestation every Congress | Article I; 5 U.S.C. § 552; 44 U.S.C. § 3506 | Congresses are newly numbered every two years. A statutory **agency-to-Congress** report can recur upon each new Congress. A statute cannot force future Congresses to certify an algorithm, adopt rules, or vote on it. |
| § 6(a)–(c), report/preserve | 18 U.S.C. §§ 2258A, 2258B, 2258C | Providers with actual knowledge of specified apparent violations have reporting duties; limitations on liability have exceptions. The safety gate must not limit or delay those provisions. |
| § 6(b)–(c), raw evidence | 18 U.S.C. § 2702 | Stored communications disclosure has restrictions and enumerated exceptions; sending raw material to a third-party telemetry service is not automatically authorized by creating an audit. Minimize and control access. |
| § 6(d), privacy | 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506; 47 U.S.C. § 230 | Preserve pre-existing children's privacy and platform moderation boundaries; not all content concerning minors is unlawful. |
| § 7, public comment and reports | 5 U.S.C. § 553; 44 U.S.C. § 3506 | Pilot rulemaking should follow applicable notice-and-comment requirements; planned information collection and disclosure require review under relevant Federal law. |
| § 7, no new licensing | U.S. Const. amend. I; *Free Speech Coalition v. Paxton*, 606 U.S. __ (2025) | The 2025 decision addresses age verification for particular sexually explicit websites. It is not authority for a universal government license to use the Internet. |
